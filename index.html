AI/ML · Computer Vision

SacredFlow — Crowd Monitoring for Temples

A hybrid crowd-monitoring pipeline developed during my internship at Amrut Centre, MNIT Jaipur. It combines density estimation for distant crowds with person detection and tracking in the near field to handle perspective differences in temple CCTV footage.

  • Combined DM-Count density estimation with YOLO detection and DeepSORT tracking.
  • Achieved a combined estimate of approximately 330–350 people per frame on the evaluated footage.
  • Tracked over 100 identities concurrently with per-person dwell-time reports.
  • Reduced per-frame computation by 76.5%, achieving ~4.3× FPS through frame skipping and caching on CPU.
